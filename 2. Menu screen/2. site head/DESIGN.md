## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive-grade, commanding enterprise aesthetic tailored for high-stakes operational intelligence and mission-critical enterprise portals. It marries modern high-density SaaS utility with timeless luxury prestige, projecting precision, authority, and institutional stability.

Targeted at C-suite executives, senior fleet and restaurant operators, and institutional directors, the interface commands absolute trust. The emotional tone is decisive, calm, and elevated.

The design movement is **Corporate Modern with High-Contrast Architectural Detailing**:
- **Pristine Grounding**: Crisp white surfaces and architectural slate underlays establish an uncluttered, high-legibility plane.
- **Deep Midnight Authority**: Inks of profound navy anchor all data points, headlines, and primary structural vectors.
- **Gilded Precision**: Metallic gold acts not as ornamentation, but as a deliberate indicator of elite status, peak performance, and critical points of action.
- **Architectural Line Work**: Defined, high-contrast borders and sharp layout delineations replace muddy drop shadows, ensuring crystalline structure across deep information hierarchies.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive hierarchy built on profound contrasts between deep obsidian-navy inks, clean architectural slates, and focused metallic gold accents.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base for primary cards, analytical viewports, and active modal workspaces.
- **Surface Muted (`#F8FAFC`)**: Secondary canvas for app shell frames, page backdrops, and side navigation gutters.
- **Surface Bordered (`#F1F5F9`)**: Neutral sub-panel backing, table header rows, and segmented control backgrounds.
- **Primary Ink (`#041329`)**: Deepest midnight navy. Used for primary displays, bold executive headlines, dominant navigation brands, and active states.
- **Secondary Ink (`#0F172A`)**: Rich dark slate navy. Used for readable body text, standard titles, and high-emphasis data points.
- **Tertiary Ink (`#475569`)**: Medium slate. Used for secondary metadata, column labels, disabled states, and timestamps.
- **Primary Accent Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: Warm metallic gold. Reserved for primary conversion buttons, selected indicators, high-tier status badges, and focal KPI achievements.
- **Deep Metallic Gold (`#B48F1E`)**: Burnished dark gold for pressed button states, focused interactive outlines, high-contrast text accents, and elevated borders.
- **Structural Border Crisp (`#E2E8F0`)**: Crisp, light boundary lines separating structural cards and analytical matrices.
- **High-Contrast Border (`#041329`)**: Used selectively for heavy architectural dividers, active focus rings, and high-impact executive card perimeters.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses corporate clarity and technological poise:
- **Display & Headlines (`Manrope`)**: Provides geometric precision, chiselled letterforms, and structural gravitas across executive dashboards and modular section headers.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly yet disciplined reading cadence with exceptional micro-legibility for dense enterprise tables and operational feeds.
- **Metrics & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Implements strict tabular alignment for currency, operational metrics, SKUs, and timeline markers.

Headlines larger than 32px automatically compress via mobile token counterparts to maintain balanced density on compact viewports without wrapping awkwardly.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an 8px base rhythm with 4px micro-increments. The layout framework relies on an asymmetrical enterprise grid tailored for multi-tier oversight:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters and 48px (`margin-desktop`) outer padding. Supports persistent 280px left-hand executive navigation rails alongside dense multi-card analytical streams.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px (`margin-tablet`) margins. Navigation shifts to a slim icon rail or slide-out drawer; analytical grids compress to 2-column aggregates.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters and 16px (`margin-mobile`) margins. Content shifts to single-column vertically stacked units with full-width telemetry cards.

Layout density is tight and utilitarian, prioritizing vertical compactness to minimize scrolling during time-sensitive command decisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural crispness and tonal surfaces rather than heavy blurred shadows.

- **Flat Architectural Baseline**: The portal prioritizes crisp 1px borders (`#E2E8F0` and `#041329`) to frame cards, ensuring no hazy blurring interferes with dense numbers and line items.
- **Tonal Layers**:
  - `Level 0 (Base Canvas)`: App shell backdrop rendered in `#F8FAFC`.
  - `Level 1 (Card & Module Deck)`: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces elevated strictly via crisp 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`).
  - `Level 2 (Dropdowns & Popovers)`: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a discreet, sharp, high-density shadow: `0px 4px 12px rgba(4, 19, 41, 0.08)` coupled with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`).
  - `Level 3 (Executive Modals & Command Bars)`: `#FFFFFF` container with deep anchor shadow: `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(4, 19, 41, 0.16), 0px 0px 0px 1px #041329`.
- **Gilded Focus Elevation**: Interactive focus and selected entities receive an inset or outer 2px boundary rendered in Gold (`#D4AF37`), signaling elite operational priority without vertical plane displacement.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects sharp, tailored architectural precision with subtle softening:
- **Base Level (`1 - Soft`)**: Core interactive elements—including inputs, standard buttons, tabular row highlights, and tags—utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- **Container Elements (`rounded-lg`)**: Primary cards, executive KPI containers, charts, and dialogs utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- **Structural Identity**: Sharp geometric silhouettes highlight the institutional nature of the portal, avoiding playful bubbly shapes in favor of serious enterprise ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep midnight navy (`#041329`) fill, white (`#FFFFFF`) text, 4px corner radius, styled with an internal top-edge highlight or a refined 1px border (`#041329`). Hover state shifts to `#0F172A` with a subtle gold accent underline or border.
- **Secondary (Executive Accent)**: Solid metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) fill with deep navy (`#041329`) typography. Active/pressed state deepens to `#B48F1E`.
- **Outline / Ghost**: 1px crisp border in `#041329` or `#E2E8F0`, pure white background, `#041329` text. Hover brings a subtle background wash of `#F1F5F9`.

### Cards & Executive Panels
- Constructed with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds framed by 1px solid `#E2E8F0` borders.
- Top-performing metrics and priority modules feature a distinctive 3px metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) top structural border highlight.
- Padding follows strict internal increments: 16px for compact modules, 24px for macro analytics.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Pure white interior, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#64748B`. Focused state engages a 1px `#041329` border with a 2px outer aura in `#D4AF37` at 30% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square/circle with a 1.5px border in `#041329`. Checked states feature `#041329` navy ground with crisp `#D4AF37` metallic checks.

### Chips & Status Badges
- High-contrast, compact dimensions (20px to 24px height), uppercase `label-md` mono-styled typography.
- Statuses utilize subtle tinted backgrounds bounded by 1px crisp borders:
  - *Optimal / Active*: `#F8FAFC` background with a 1px `#D4AF37` border and `#B48F1E` text.
  - *Standard Navy*: `#F1F5F9` background with a 1px `#041329` border and `#041329` text.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers use `#F8FAFC` background with subtle uppercase tracking in `#475569` and a prominent 1px bottom border in `#041329`.
- Rows leverage alternating hover states in `#F8FAFC` with crisp dividing borders in `#F1F5F9`.
- Numerical columns strictly employ `data-mono` typography aligned to the right.

### Compass Navigation Matrix (Specialized Widget)
- Directional nodes (Order, Prep, Serve, Data) utilize deep navy circular markers accented with 1px concentric rings of `#D4AF37`.
- Center cutlery/compass crosshairs incorporate dual-tone `#041329` and `#D4AF37` facet shading for tactile precision.
