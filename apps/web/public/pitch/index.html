01 · The problem
Where most chains are stuck

10+ disconnected point solutions.
No single source of truth.

A typical multi-location group runs a patchwork of tools that never talk to each other — and every gap becomes a leak, a delay, or a standards failure.

POS / ordering Kitchen display Staff shifts Payments Maintenance CMMS Quality audits Digital signage Customer surveys Analytics Inventory
  • Integration overhead — every new tool needs plumbing to every other tool; most pairs never get connected
  • Data silos — a dish can't flow from menu to kitchen to payment to analytics inside one system
  • No cross-branch visibility — head office can't compare sites, standards, or revenue hygiene in real time
  • Zero integration = zero trust — anomalies hide in the seams between systems

02 · The platform
What OmniOps is

One login. One database.
Real-time cross-module data.

OmniOps replaces the patchwork with one integrated operations platform — built multi-tenant from day one, for groups of 10 to 500+ outlets.

  • One login, one console — every module, every site, every role in a single system
  • Real-time by design — an order placed at the counter reaches the kitchen display, payment, revenue assurance and analytics without a single integration
  • Owner console + central team + site teams — one hierarchy, granular role-based access
  • Modules à la carte — switch capabilities on/off per site, per brand

06 · Trust
Security & multi-tenancy

Role-based access, enforced at the API — not just the UI

  • Tenant isolation verified — a site team literally cannot read another site's data; every query is scoped server-side
  • RBAC at the API layer — guards on every endpoint, roles in the JWT, not a frontend flag
  • Central vs site roles — brand manager sees the network; a site lead sees one site
